Revitalizing Kidney and Cancer Care.

Aadhiran Speciality Clinic is a patient- centric, speciality clinic dedicated to comprehensive Nephrology and Medical Oncology services. It is located in the heart of Madurai city at Ellis Nagar. It is led by young and energetic Dr P Vathsalyan & Dr S Sivayogini. We focus on ethical, evidence based, quality medical care with early diagnosis and prevention of kidney and cancer related diseases.
